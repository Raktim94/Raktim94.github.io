FIELD NOTE 13 · KINETIRX

Data Minimization Is a Product Feature Even When It Is Not on the Screen

CareCloud’s 2026 SEC filing described a temporary network disruption affecting one EHR environment; later filings and notices updated the scope of affected people. Healthcare incidents show why access and retention boundaries matter.

By Raktim Ranjit · 27 October 2026 · 5 min read

The incident, and the question it raises

A clinic or pharmacy should ask what patient information is necessary for the job, who can see it, how long it is retained, and what the restore plan looks like. Role-based access and a clear retention policy help, but they need to match local law and actual clinical workflows.

Where KinetiRx fits

KinetiRx documents patient records, visit history, role-based staff permissions, and configurable invoice retention. It is self-hosted, so the operator can choose where the database lives and how long records are kept. Those controls are useful only when configured and governed well.

A clear boundary

Self-hosting does not make data private by itself. KinetiRx is not a compliance certification; assess applicable privacy and health-record laws and secure the deployment.
