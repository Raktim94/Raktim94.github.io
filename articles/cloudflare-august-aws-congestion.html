FIELD NOTE 19 · ORDERRESTRO

A Tunnel Adds Reach—and Another Dependency to Your Diagram

Cloudflare’s August 2025 incident report described congestion on links between Cloudflare and AWS US-EAST-1 during a traffic surge. The failure crossed a provider boundary rather than fitting one simple “cloud down” label.

By Raktim Ranjit · 27 October 2026 · 5 min read

The incident, and the question it raises

Hybrid architectures can fail at the seams: a tunnel connects providers, a DNS record points to an edge, and a business app sits behind a cloud network. Each layer may be healthy on its own while the path between them degrades. Keep a diagram that includes those edges, not just boxes.

Where OrderRestro fits

OrderRestro documents local-LAN operation plus optional remote access through tunnels or reverse proxies. Staff can use the local address for in-house work; remote access should be treated as a separate convenience and tested as such.

A clear boundary

The tunnel option remains dependent on its provider and upstream network. Do not expose an unauthenticated admin panel to the internet; use secure transport and appropriate access controls.
