A Phone Call Can Be an Attack Path Around a Strong Firewall

Abbott said its 2026 incident involved vishing and affected a limited number of internal systems in a business unit; it reported no impact to patient-facing operations.

By Raktim Ranjit · 27 October 2026 · 5 min read

The incident, and the question it raises

Social engineering targets people and trust processes, not just servers. A small business can make the next step safer with unique staff accounts, least privilege, an approval rule for unusual requests, and a simple way to report a suspicious call before anyone shares a code or resets an account.

Where OrderRestro fits

OrderRestro’s public project describes role-based access with granular permissions across operational modules. That allows an owner to give a cashier the work they need without handing every staff account administrator powers.

A clear boundary

Role-based access limits some mistakes; it cannot stop someone from voluntarily sharing a credential or approving a fraudulent request. Pair software permissions with staff training and account protections.
