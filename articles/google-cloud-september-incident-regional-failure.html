FIELD NOTE 20 · NODEDR POS

A Region Can Be Healthy on the Dashboard and Still Be Your Single Point of Failure

Google Cloud recorded a September 2026 incident lasting over four hours across several services in europe-west2. Regional availability claims only matter when workloads and recovery paths are designed around them.

By Raktim Ranjit · 27 October 2026 · 5 min read

The incident, and the question it raises

A multi-region architecture is not achieved by checking a box in a hosting console. Data replication, failover behavior, secrets, DNS, and human runbooks all have to work together. Small businesses should also ask a simpler question: what minimum capability must remain available when the remote host cannot be reached?

Where NodeDR POS fits

NodeDR POS can be run on a shop machine for local checkout and inventory, or on a VPS for remote management. The deployment choice should follow the shop’s operating needs and staff capacity rather than a slogan about cloud or local hosting.

A clear boundary

Local installations can fail too. Plan for power, disk failure, theft, and recovery; use tested backups and keep a simple manual fallback for sales.
