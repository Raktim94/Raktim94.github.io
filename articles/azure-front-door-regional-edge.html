FIELD NOTE 07 · NODEDR POS

A CDN Is Part of the Product When the Login Page Needs It

Microsoft’s Azure status history documented Front Door and CDN connectivity issues across multiple regions in October 2025. Edge services can become a user-facing dependency even when application servers are healthy.

By Raktim Ranjit · 27 October 2026 · 5 min read

The incident, and the question it raises

The edge layer is often introduced to improve reach, caching, or security. But if the primary application is only accessible through that layer, an edge incident may make the business workflow unavailable. Teams should map which paths can be served locally and which intentionally depend on a remote edge.

Where NodeDR POS fits

NodeDR POS can run on a shop machine and be accessed from the same Wi-Fi network, while the same stack can also be hosted on a VPS. This gives operators a deployment choice for checkout and inventory rather than a single assumed hosting model.

A clear boundary

Do not infer that the product bypasses Azure Front Door or any CDN in every setup. A VPS, tunnel, domain resolver, and online integrations may still depend on remote services.
