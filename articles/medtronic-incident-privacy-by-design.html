FIELD NOTE 15 · KINETIRX

A Security Incident Is Also a Test of Your Data Boundaries

Medtronic reported in 2026 that a cyber incident had affected some corporate IT systems and that it was contacting people whose information might have been impacted.

By Raktim Ranjit · 27 October 2026 · 5 min read

The incident, and the question it raises

For a pharmacy system, privacy questions should be settled before an incident: which records are stored, who can export them, which devices can access the admin account, and which information should never be copied into a third-party AI prompt?

Where KinetiRx fits

KinetiRx’s repository documents server-side JWT authorization and role checks, and its optional Gemini workflow is separate from the core offline mode. Operators can choose not to enable AI and should review the exact information sent to any provider.

A clear boundary

The product is not a medical-device control system and does not certify a pharmacy’s privacy posture. Keep sensitive records out of public repositories and review provider terms and local requirements.
