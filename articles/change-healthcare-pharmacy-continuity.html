FIELD NOTE 16 · KINETIRX

The Pharmacy Counter and the Claims Network Are Not the Same System

The 2024 Change Healthcare cyberattack disrupted claims and payment operations across the US health system. HHS described monitoring effects on access to care and concern for smaller practices.

By Raktim Ranjit · 27 October 2026 · 5 min read

The incident, and the question it raises

The lesson for independent pharmacies is to separate local work from external settlement. A local system can record a sale, but it cannot make a payer’s claims switch, eligibility service, or reimbursement network available when that external service is down. A good continuity checklist names each dependency and the reconciliation step.

Where KinetiRx fits

KinetiRx documents billing, mixed payment modes, a due-khata ledger, order tracking, and drawer reconciliation. These can help organize local pharmacy activity, but the product is not a replacement for insurer claims infrastructure or a guarantee of payer connectivity.

A clear boundary

Do not describe a local POS as a cure for a national claims outage. Confirm which transactions can be recorded offline and how they will be reconciled with outside networks.
